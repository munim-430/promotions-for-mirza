## ⚖️ Governing Legal Doctrines & Statutory Enforceability

### 1. Mandatory 50% Provisional Advance (상법 제732조의2 가지급보험금)
* **Statutory Rule:** Commercial Code §732-2 & Standard Automobile Insurance Policy Art. 34(3).
* **Legal Obligation:** Once accidental death is proven (via Hospital Death Certificate & Police Body Release Order), DB Insurance **MUST** pay at least 50% of the estimated death benefit (**KRW 50,000,000**) within 10 business days.
* **No Investigation Exception:** The insurer cannot withhold provisional benefits pending further accident investigation.

### 2. Preemption of Unlicensed Driving Exclusion (대법원 98다48963 전원합의체)
* **Landmark En Banc Precedent:** Supreme Court En Banc Decision 98Da48963 (*대법원 1998. 12. 22. 선고 98다48963 전원합의체 판결*).
* **Commercial Code §732-2:** An insurer cannot exclude liability for accidental death caused by the gross negligence (*중대한 과실*) of the insured. Unlicensed driving is classified as gross negligence, NOT intentional self-harm (*고의*).
* **Commercial Code §663 (불이익변경금지원칙):** Any policy term less favorable to the insured than statutory law is null and void.
* **Absolute Invalidation:** DB Insurance's standard unlicensed driving exclusion clause is **void ab initio** regarding Self-Physical Accident death benefits.

### 3. Cross-Border Succession & Inherent Property Doctrine (국제사법 제49조)
