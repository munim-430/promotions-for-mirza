- **Forensic Stress-Test Analysis**:
  1. Under Bangladeshi Private International Law (following common law conflict of laws rules), succession to movable property and personal claims is governed by the deceased's *lex domicilii* (law of domicile).
  2. The deceased was a temporary international student in Korea under a D-2 visa. Under private international law, a student does not acquire a domicile of choice abroad because they lack the *animus manendi* (intent to remain indefinitely); their domicile of origin in Bangladesh remains intact.

Korean law treats the reference to "statutory heirs" in the insurance contract as an identification of the beneficiaries determined by the national law of the insured.
