## 2. Pillar I: Private International Law Act Art. 49 & Bangladeshi Muslim Succession Architecture

### 2.1 Private International Law Act (국제사법) Article 49 Mechanics

The Republic of Korea Private International Law Act (Act No. 18671) establishes the conflict of laws rules governing multi-jurisdictional successions:

```
[국제사법 제49조(상속)]
① 상속은 사망 당시 피상속인의 본국법에 의한다.
② 피상속인은 유언에 정하는 방식에 따라 다음 각 호의 어느 하나에 해당하는 법을 지정하여 상속을 준거법으로 할 수 있다. 다만, 유류분에 관한 권리는 피상속인의 사망 당시 본국법에 의한다.
   1. 지정 당시 피상속인의 상거소가 있는 국가의 법
   2. 부동산에 관한 상속의 경우 그 부동산이 소재하는 국가의 법
```

#### Application to the Present Case:
1. **Intestate Lex Patriae**: The deceased Mahadi Monoar Hossain executed no testamentary disposition or choice-of-law designation under Article 49(2). Therefore, Paragraph 1 mandates that **the law of Bangladesh** (피상속인의 본국법) exclusively governs all matters of succession.
2. **Scope of the Governing Law**: The lex successionis determines:
   - Capacity of the deceased to transmit rights;

   - Fractional shares of each heir (상속지분율);
   - Devolution of claims, personal accident death benefits, and damages for wrongful death.

### 2.2 Substantive Bangladeshi Muslim Succession Law (Sunni Hanafi Jurisprudence)

In Bangladesh, succession for Muslim citizens is governed by personal religious law pursuant to **The Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937** and the **Succession Act, 1925**. Bangladeshi Muslims predominantly adhere to the **Sunni Hanafi School of Jurisprudence** (*Sirajiyyah* rules of inheritance).

#### Heirship Classification Scheme:
Under Hanafi jurisprudence, heirs are categorized into three primary classes:
1. **Sharers (*Zawil Furud* / 12 Quranic Heirs)**: Heirs entitled to fixed statutory fractions set forth in the Holy Quran.
2. **Residuaries (*Asaba* / Agnatic Heirs)**: Heirs who take the entire residue of the estate after all Sharers have received their fixed shares.
3. **Distant Kindred (*Zawil Arham*)**: Blood relations who inherit only in the total absence of Sharers and Residuaries.

### 2.4 Legal Characterization of Insurance Payouts: Estate Asset vs Original Right of Heirs

Under established Korean Supreme Court precedents (**Supreme Court Decision 2003Da29463 rendered July 9, 2004**; **Supreme Court Decision 2000Da31502 rendered December 28, 2001**):
1. In first-party automobile accident death insurance (자기신체사고 / 자동차상해) and group accident death insurance (단체상해보험) where the beneficiary is designated as "법정상속인" (statutory heirs) or left unspecified:
   - The death benefit claim right does **not** form part of the deceased's general insolvent estate subject to general creditors.
   - The claim right is acquired **directly and originally by the statutory heirs as their inherent, original right (상속인의 고유재산)**.
2. The entitlement ratio among multiple statutory heirs follows the statutory inheritance share designated by the governing succession law (PIL Art. 49 -> Bangladeshi Law: 2/3 to Father, 1/3 to Mother).

### 3.2 Dual-Track Strategic Legalization Architecture

#### Track A: Hague Apostille Step-by-Step Procedure:
1. **Local Drafting & Notarization**: Draft the document (Succession Certificate, Family Certificate, Special POA) in English (or bilingual Korean/English) and execute before a licensed Notary Public in Bangladesh.
2. **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Apostille Attestation**: Submit the notarized document to the Consular & Official Documents Section, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Segunbagicha, Dhaka. MOFA affixes the standardized Hague Apostille Certificate (All-in-One Digital/Physical Apostille Stamp).
3. **Delivery to Korea**: Transmit the original Apostilled document to Korea.

#### Track B: Consular Legalization (전통적 영사확인) Step-by-Step Procedure:
1. **Notarization**: Notarization by a registered Bangladeshi Notary Public.
2. **MOFA Attestation**: Attestation by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh (Dhaka).
3. **Korean Embassy Consular Legalization**: Submission to the Consular Section of the **Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Dhaka, Bangladesh** (주방글라데시 대한민국 대사관, Madani Avenue, Baridhara, Dhaka-1212) for Consular Attestation / Legalization (영사확인 직인).

### 4.1 Legal Mandate under Korean Civil Act Article 118 & Special Authority Requirements

Under Korean Civil Act Article 118 (Scope of Agent's Authority), an agent with general authority may only perform acts of management or preservation. An agent cannot compromise, settle, waive claims, initiate dispute mediation, or collect and remit large financial assets without **Express Special Authority (특별수권)**.

```
[민법 제118조(대리권의 범위)]
권한을 정하지 아니한 대리인은 다음 각 호의 행위만을 할 수 있다.
1. 보존행위
2. 대리의 목적인 물건이나 권리의 성질을 변하지 아니하는 범위에서 그 이용 또는 개량하는 행위
```
