## 2. Comprehensive Statutory & Jurisprudential Audit

### 2.1 The "Statutory Trinity" of the Korean Commercial Code
The core legal thesis across all three claim documents rests on the interlocking operation of Commercial Code Articles 732-2, 739, and 663:

| Statutory Provision | Text & Legal Mandate | Application in Claim Package | Audit Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Commercial Code Art. 732-2** (중과실로 인한 보험사고의 보상) | In insurance contracts covering death, the insurer **cannot be exempt from liability** even if the accident was caused by the gross negligence (*중대한 과실*) of the policyholder, insured, or beneficiary. Only intentional acts (*고의*) relieve the insurer. | Established that driving without a valid local license constitutes gross negligence, NOT intentional suicide/willful harm. | **PASSED (100% Compliant)** |
| **Commercial Code Art. 739** (상해보험에의 준용) | Provisions governing life insurance (including Art. 732-2) **apply mutatis mutandis to personal accident insurance** (*상해보험*). | Direct application to first-party auto accident coverage (*자기신체사고 / 자동차상해*) and university group accident insurance. | **PASSED (100% Compliant)** |
| **Commercial Code Art. 663** (보험계약자 등의 불이익변경금지원칙) | Provisions of Part IV (Insurance) **shall not be altered by special agreement** to the disadvantage of the policyholder, insured, or beneficiary (*상대적 강행규정성*). | Proves that any policy term or rider purporting to exempt unlicensed driving in death coverage is **null and void ab initio** (*절대적·당연무효*). | **PASSED (100% Compliant)** |
