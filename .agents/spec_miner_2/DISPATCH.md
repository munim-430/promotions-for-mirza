### Objective
Analyze the Korean legal framework, statutory provisions, standard auto insurance clauses, and binding Supreme Court precedents governing first-party accidental death benefits (자기신체사고 / 자동차상해) and the invalidity of the unlicensed driving exemption (무면허운전 면책약관의 무효성).

### Detailed Areas of Analysis
1. Korean Commercial Code (상법) Provisions:
   - Article 732-2 (Accidental Death / Self-Inflicted Injury Exception - only gross negligence vs intentional suicide; insurer cannot exempt for gross negligence in accidental death)
   - Article 739 (Application mutatis mutandis to accident insurance)
   - Article 663 (Prohibition of Disadvantageous Alteration to Policyholder/Insured - 강행규정 / 불이익변경금지 원칙)
2. Binding Supreme Court Precedents (대법원 판례):
   - Supreme Court Decision 98Da48963 (대법원 1998. 12. 22. 선고 98다48963 판결) - Invalidity of unlicensed driving exemption in first-party accidental death / personal accident coverage.
   - Supreme Court Decision 97Da27039 (대법원 1998. 4. 24. 선고 97다27039 판결) - Principle that unlicensed driving exemption in first-party injury insurance violates Commercial Code Art. 732-2 & Art. 663 and is null and void.
   - Supreme Court Decision 98Da26910 (대법원 1998. 10. 27. 선고 98다26910 판결) - Unlicensed driving exclusion clause validity boundary and distinction between first-party accidental death and third-party liability indemnity.
3. Standard Automobile Insurance Terms (자동차보험 표준약관):
   - Definition of Self-Physical Accident (자기신체사고) / Auto Injury (자동차상해).
   - Distinction between Third-Party Liability (대인배상) where unlicensed driving may require co-payment or exemption, vs First-Party Accidental Death (자손/자상) where unlicensed exemption is legally VOID as a matter of mandatory statutory law.
4. Statutory Deadlines & Interest:
   - Insurance Business Act & standard terms: 3 business days from receipt of claim or 10 business days for investigation; default interest rates (상사법정이율 6% per annum under Commercial Code Art. 54, and Special Act on Promotion of Litigation Act 12% per annum under 소송촉진등에관한특례법).
5. FSS Dispute Mediation Standards:
   - Financial Supervisory Service (금융감독원) mediation procedure, petition format, basis under Financial Consumer Protection Act (금융소비자보호법).
