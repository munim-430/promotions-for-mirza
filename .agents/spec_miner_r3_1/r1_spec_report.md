## 3.3 Governing Succession Law (PIL Art. 49) & Mathematical Apportionment

Under **Article 49(1) of the Korean Private International Law Act (국제사법 제49조 제1항)**, intestate succession is governed by the national law of the deceased (*lex patriae*). The deceased Mahadi Monoar Hossain was an unmarried Bangladeshi citizen who died intestate:
1. **Governing Law:** Muslim Personal Law (Sunni Hanafi Jurisprudence) as applied in Bangladesh.
2. **Mother (MONOARA BEGUM):** In the absence of children/descendants, takes a fixed Quranic Share (*Zawil Furud*, Surah An-Nisa 4:11) of **1/3 (33.333%)**.
3. **Father (BELAL HOSSAIN):** In the absence of children, inherits as the supreme Agnatic Residuary (*Asaba*), taking the **remaining 2/3 (66.667%)**.
4. **Total Exclusion:** All collateral relatives (brothers, sisters, uncles) are entirely excluded (*Mahjub*).
5. **Inherent Original Property Doctrine:** Under **Supreme Court Decision 2003Da29463**, first-party accidental death benefits belong originally and inheringly to the statutory heirs (*상속인의 고유재산*), bypassing estate debts, claims, or third-party subrogation.

## 4.1 Mathematical Computation Formulas

1. **Pre-Litigation Commercial Default Interest ($I_{\text{commercial}}$):**
   $$I_{\text{commercial}} = P \times r_{\text{commercial}} \times \left( \frac{D_{\text{commercial}}}{365} \right) = 100,000,000 \times 0.06 \times \left( \frac{D_{\text{commercial}}}{365} \right)$$
   $$\text{Daily Accrual} = \frac{100,000,000 \times 0.06}{365} = \text{KRW } 16,438.356 \text{ per day}$$

2. **Litigation Penalty Default Interest ($I_{\text{litigation}}$):**
   $$I_{\text{litigation}} = P \times r_{\text{litigation}} \times \left( \frac{D_{\text{litigation}}}{365} \right) = 100,000,000 \times 0.12 \times \left( \frac{D_{\text{litigation}}}{365} \right)$$
   $$\text{Daily Accrual} = \frac{100,000,000 \times 0.12}{365} = \text{KRW } 32,876.712 \text{ per day}$$

3. **Total Enforceable Claim Amount ($A_{\text{total}}$):**
   $$A_{\text{total}} = P + I_{\text{commercial}} + I_{\text{litigation}} + C_{\text{legal}}$$
   *(Where $P = \text{KRW } 100,000,000$, and $C_{\text{legal}}$ represents recoverable statutory court costs and attorney fee indemnification under the Court Costs Act).*
