### 2.1 Defense Counsel's Five Potential Attack Theories & Comprehensive Rebuttals

- **Insurer's Hypothetical Argument:** Samsung Fire's standard automobile insurance terms contain an explicit exclusion rider stating that damages arising while the insured operates a vehicle without a driver's license shall not be indemnified.
- **Adversarial Stress-Test & Rebuttal:**
  1. Under **Commercial Code Article 732-2 (상법 제732조의2)**, in any insurance contract where death is the insured event, the insurer is statutorily prohibited from asserting exemption even if the accident was caused by the gross negligence (*중대한 과실*) of the insured.
  2. Under **Commercial Code Article 739 (상법 제739조)**, all life insurance protective rules apply mutatis mutandis to personal accident insurance (*상해보험*), which includes first-party Self-Physical Accident (*자기신체사고*) and Auto Personal Injury (*자동차상해*) coverages.
  3. Under **Commercial Code Article 663 (상법 제663조 - 불이익변경금지원칙)**, standard policy terms cannot alter statutory protections to the detriment of the insured or beneficiaries.
  4. In **Supreme Court Decision 98Da48963 (Decided Dec. 22, 1998)**, the Supreme Court ruled unequivocally:
     > *"The unlicensed driving exemption clause in automobile insurance special agreements... insofar as it applies to first-party self-physical accident coverage, violates Articles 732-2, 739, and 663 of the Commercial Code and is STRICTLY NULL AND VOID."*
  5. The defense collapses completely as a matter of mandatory statutory law.

#### Attack Theory 1.2: Gross Negligence vs. Intentional Act Distinction (상법 제659조 유추 주장)
- **Insurer's Hypothetical Argument:** Operating a vehicle without a license is a deliberate violation of the Road Traffic Act and should be treated as equivalent to an intentional act (*고의*) under Commercial Code Art. 659 rather than mere gross negligence.
- **Adversarial Stress-Test & Rebuttal:**
  1. Korean civil and insurance jurisprudence maintains a strict distinction between *intentional commission of an illegal act* (e.g., driving without a license) and *intentional infliction of the insured harm* (e.g., intentional self-destruction / suicide).
  2. In **Supreme Court Decision 98Da26910 (Decided Oct. 27, 1998)**, the Court expressly held:
     > *"Even if an accident resulted from the insured's unlicensed driving, the insurer cannot assert exemption from liability unless the accident was caused by the intentional act (willful suicide) of the insured."*
  3. Unlicensed driving is classified under settled law as gross negligence, never as intentional self-harm.

  1. *자배법* Article 29-2 applies exclusively to **Third-Party Bodily Injury Liability (대인배상 I·II)** and **Third-Party Property Damage Liability (대물배상)** to compensate innocent third-party victims while preventing tortfeasors from evading civil accountability.

  3. First-party Self-Physical Accident / Auto Injury coverage is a **first-party personal accident insurance contract (인보험)**, completely outside the scope of *자배법* third-party recourse provisions. Zero co-payment or deduction is legally permissible.

### 3.1 Strict Burden of Proof under Korean Supreme Court Jurisprudence
Under established Supreme Court precedents (**Supreme Court Decision 2001Da55453 rendered March 29, 2002**; **Supreme Court Decision 2008Da81633 rendered May 13, 2010**; **Supreme Court Decision 2001Da49628 rendered Sept. 28, 2001**):
1. **Burden on Insurer**: The burden of pleading and proving that an insured event was caused by the deliberate intentional act or suicide of the insured rests **entirely upon the insurer**.
2. **Standard of Proof**: The insurer must prove intentional suicide to a degree of certainty that eliminates all reasonable doubt in accordance with ordinary social experience (*사회통념상 의심의 여지가 없을 정도로 명백하게 증명하여야 함*).
3. **Presumption Against Suicide**: In the absence of decisive evidence (e.g., suicide note, explicit prior suicidal declarations, extreme psychiatric history, intentional steering into an abyss with zero evasive action), the death is legally presumed to be an unintended, accidental occurrence (*우발적 사고*).
