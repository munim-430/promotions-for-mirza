## 4. CHECK 2: PRECEDENT & STATUTORY CITATION AUTHENTICITY

### 4.1 Supreme Court En Banc Decision 98Da48963 (대법원 1998. 12. 22. 선고 98다48963 전원합의체 판결)
- **Subject:** Absolute invalidity of unlicensed driving exemption clauses (*무면허운전 면책약관*) in first-party accidental death insurance (*자기신체사고 / 상해보험*).
- **Court Holding:** Automobile first-party accidental death coverage has the legal character of personal accident insurance (*상해보험*). Under Commercial Code Article 732-2 and Article 663, insurers cannot exempt themselves from liability for accidental death caused by gross negligence (*중과실*). Unlicensed driving constitutes gross negligence, not intentional suicide (*고의에 의한 자기사망*). Therefore, policy terms excluding unlicensed driving from accidental death benefits are **strictly null and void**.
- **Audit Verification:** Correctly analyzed and cited in all 13 documents as the primary legal cornerstone against Samsung Fire.

### 4.2 Supreme Court Decision 97Da27039 (대법원 1997. 11. 14. 선고 97다27039 판결)
