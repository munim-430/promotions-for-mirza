# Evidence Checklist

## Already preserved

The curated repository evidence folder contains police, hospital, embassy/consular, identity and repatriation-related source documents. Original documents control over summaries.

## Obtain before drafting the claim

- Seojeong/insurer master policy certificate.
- Complete policy wording and endorsements applicable on the accident date.
- Benefit schedule.
- Individual enrollment/insured-person confirmation.
- Insurer claim form and claims contact.
- Claim deadline.
- Insurer's written list of required heir documents.
- Insurer's written list of authority/POA documents if a representative files.

## Authority documents

Before using a power of attorney or directing payment to a representative, confirm that it was actually executed, satisfies any required authentication/translation rules, and is accepted by the insurer.

## Do not include unless verified

- any estimated payout range;
- any DB automobile death-benefit demand;
- any provisional-payment amount;
- any guaranteed-payment statement;
- any assumed inheritance percentage;
- any legal precedent not checked against the exact policy issue.
