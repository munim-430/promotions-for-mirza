### 가. 상법 제732조의2, 제739조 및 제663조의 강행규정 위반
1. **상법 제732조의2 (중과실로 인한 보험사고):**
   상법 제732조의2는 *"사망을 보험사고로 한 보험계약에서 사고가 보험계약자 또는 피보험자나 보험수익자의 중대한 과실로 인하여 발생한 경우에도 보험자는 보험금을 지급할 책임을 면하지 못한다"*고 규정하고 있습니다.
   상법 제659조의 일반 면책사유(고의 또는 중과실)와 달리, 인명 피해가 발생하는 사망보험에서는 입법자가 정책적으로 보험자의 면책 범위를 '오직 고의(자살 등)에 의한 사고'로 엄격히 제한하고, **중과실로 인한 사고에 대해서는 어떠한 경우에도 면책을 허용하지 않도록 강행법규화**한 것입니다.

2. **상법 제739조 (상해보험에의 준용):**
   상법 제739조는 상해보험에 관하여 생명보험에 관한 규정을 준용하도록 규정하고 있으므로, **상법 제732조의2는 상해보험인 자기신체사고(자손) 및 자동차상해(자상) 담보에 그대로 준용**됩니다.

3. **상법 제663조 (불이익변경금지원칙):**
   상법 제663조 본문은 *"이 편의 규정은 당사자간의 특약으로 보험계약자 또는 피보험자나 보험수익자의 불이익으로 변경하지 못한다"*고 명시하고 있습니다.
   따라서 상법 제732조의2 및 제739조의 강행규정에 위반하여, 피보험자의 중과실에 불과한 '무면허운전'을 이유로 사망보험금 지급 책임을 면제하도록 규정한 자동차보험 약관조항은 **상법 제663조에 의하여 당사자 간의 효력이 전면 부인되는 절대적·당연 무효(Null and Void ab initio)**입니다.

   > *"In an insurance contract where death is the insured event, even if the accident occurs due to the gross negligence of the policyholder, the insured, or the insurance beneficiary, the insurer shall not be exempt from the liability to pay the insurance amount."*
   The Korean National Assembly enacted Article 732-2 as a mandatory public policy shield. While intentional suicide (고의) relieves an insurer of liability, **gross negligence (중과실) cannot legally exempt the insurer**. Under Korean tort and insurance doctrine, operating a motor vehicle without a valid local license constitutes gross negligence, not intentional self-destruction.

2. **Article 739 (Provisions Applicable Mutatis Mutandis - 상법 제739조):**
   > *"With respect to accident insurance, the provisions concerning life insurance shall apply mutatis mutandis..."*
   By explicit statutory mandate, Article 732-2 applies directly to all personal accident coverages (자기신체사고 및 자동차상해 담보).

3. **Article 663 (Prohibition of Disadvantageous Alteration - 상법 제663조):**
   > *"The provisions of this Part [Part IV Insurance] shall not be altered by special agreement between the parties to the disadvantage of the policyholder, the insured, or the insurance beneficiary..."*
   Any standard policy term, rider, or endorsement that purports to relieve DB Insurance of accidental death liability on grounds of unlicensed driving alters statutory protections to the detriment of the insured and is **rendered completely null and void ab initio**.

### B. Settled, Binding Supreme Court Case Law

#### 1) Supreme Court Decision 98Da48963 (Decided Dec. 22, 1998) — The Definitive Landmark Case
> **[Verbatim Legal Holding]**  
> *"Pursuant to Articles 732-2, 739, and 663 of the Commercial Code, in an insurance contract covering death or injury, the insurer shall not be exempt from liability even if the accident occurred due to the gross negligence of the insured. Because these provisions are mandatory rules of law, any policy clause exempting the insurer for an insured event caused by gross negligence is entirely void.*  
> ***The unlicensed driving exemption clause in an automobile insurance special agreement, which provides that the insurer shall not indemnify damages arising when the insured operates a vehicle without a driver's license, is a clause exempting the insurer for an accident caused by the gross negligence of unlicensed driving; therefore, insofar as it applies to first-party self-physical accident coverage (자기신체사고), it violates Articles 732-2, 739, and 663 of the Commercial Code and is STRICTLY NULL AND VOID.***"*

#### 2) Supreme Court Decision 97Da27039 (Decided April 24, 1998)
> **[Verbatim Legal Holding]**  
> *"First-party self-physical accident coverage under standard automobile insurance policy terms pays insurance proceeds when the insured suffers bodily injury arising out of the ownership, use, or management of the insured vehicle, and thus possesses the **inherent legal character of personal accident insurance (인보험/상해보험)**.*  
> *Therefore, the mandatory rules of Commercial Code Articles 732-2, 739, and 663 apply directly to self-physical accident coverage. **Consequently, any policy clause exempting the insurer from liability for an accident caused by the insured's unlicensed driving violates Commercial Code Articles 732-2, 739, and 663 and is NULL AND VOID.***"*

#### 3) Supreme Court Decision 98Da26910 (Decided Oct. 27, 1998)
> **[Verbatim Legal Holding]**  
> *"First-party self-physical accident coverage in an automobile insurance contract is a form of personal accident insurance and is governed by Commercial Code Articles 732-2, 739, and 663; **therefore, even if the accident resulted from the insured's unlicensed driving, the insurer cannot assert exemption from liability unless the accident was caused by the intentional act (willful suicide) of the insured.***"*

#### 4) Supreme Court En Banc Decision 97Da48753 & Constitutional Court Decision 95Heonba39
The Supreme Court En Banc and the Constitutional Court have repeatedly affirmed that mandatory Commercial Code provisions supersede standard insurance terms, cementing the absolute invalidity of unlicensed driving defenses in accidental death claims.
