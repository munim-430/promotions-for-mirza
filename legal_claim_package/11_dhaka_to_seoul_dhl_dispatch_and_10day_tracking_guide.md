### 1.5 Legal Proof of Delivery (POD) & Korean Civil Act Art. 111 Service Rules

Under Korean law, service of legal demands and notifications of claim are strictly governed by the **Arrival Doctrine (*도달주의*, Korean Civil Act Art. 111)**:

1. **Civil Act Article 111(1) (Time of Occurrence of Effect of Declaration of Intention):**
   > *"A declaration of intention made to another person shall take effect at the time when it reaches the other party." (상대방 있는 의사표시는 상대방에게 도달한 때에 그 효력이 생긴다.)*
2. **Legal Determination of Arrival (*도달의 요건*):**
   - Arrival does not require personal reading by the corporate CEO; delivery to the corporation's registered office mailroom, claims receptionist, or designated agent during normal business hours constitutes completed legal service (Supreme Court 82DaKa1748).
3. **Execution Steps upon DHL Delivery:**
   - As soon as the DHL tracking status updates to **"DELIVERED - SIGNED FOR BY [RECIPIENT NAME]"**, the attorney-in-fact must immediately download the official **DHL Electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD)** PDF containing the delivery timestamp, consignee signature, and GPS delivery coordinates.
   - The date of delivery recorded on the e-POD represents **Day 0 (도달일)**.
   - The **10-Day Statutory Provisional Payment Clock** begins running on the immediate next calendar day (**Day 1**).

### 3.1 Statutory Framework, Calculation Rules & Arrival Doctrine (도달주의)

1. **Statutory 50% Provisional Advance Mandate (*가지급보험금 지급의무*):**
   - **Governing Clause:** Standard Auto Insurance Terms Article 34(3) & Commercial Code Art. 732-2.
   - **Statutory Rule:** When a claim for accidental death benefit is filed and investigation is ongoing, the insurer **MUST** disburse 50% of the estimated insurance benefit (**KRW 50,000,000**) within **10 business days** of receiving the claim (or within 3 business days of medical/police document verification).
2. **Statutory Day Count Computation (*기산점 및 기간 계산*):**
   - **Civil Act Article 157 (First Day Excluded / 초일불산입 원칙):** The date of certified arrival (Day 0) is excluded from the calculation. The 10-day period begins on Day 1.
   - **Civil Act Article 161 (Holiday / Weekend Extension):** If the 10th day falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or official Korean national holiday, the statutory deadline extends to the immediate next business day.
