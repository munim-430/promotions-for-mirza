## 2. KOREA LEGAL AID CORPORATION (대한법률구조공단 - KLAC) FOREIGNER LEGAL AID SCHEME

### 2.1 Statutory & Regulatory Basis

1. **Governing Law:** Legal Aid Act (법률구조법 - Act No. 18498), Arts. 1, 2, 7, 8, and 22.
2. **Internal Regulations:**
   - Korea Legal Aid Corporation Legal Aid Regulations (대한법률구조공단 법률구조규칙).
   - Litigation Representation Operational Rules (소송대리업무규정).
   - Foreigner Legal Aid Scheme Operational Directives (외국인 법률구조제도 운영지침).
3. **Public Mission:** Established under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Justice (법무부) to provide free legal advice, document preparation, and civil litigation representation to economically disadvantaged persons and legally vulnerable foreign residents in the Republic of Korea.
4. **Foreign National Inclusivity Mandate:** Under Legal Aid Act Art. 22 and Ministry of Justice Directives, foreign nationals whose causes of action arise from accidents, contracts, or torts committed within South Korean territory are fully entitled to free civil litigation aid on an equal footing with Korean citizens.

### 2.2 Foreigner Eligibility Criteria & Low-Income Standing

1. **Standard Income Ceiling (기준 중위소득 125% 이하):**
   - General criteria mandate that an applicant’s monthly household income must be equal to or less than **125% of the Korean National Median Income (기준 중위소득)**.
   - For a single-member or low-income foreign family, this standard ensures that indigent individuals are completely protected against legal costs.
2. **Special Target Group Eligibility (특수 지원 대상):**
   - **Deceased Foreign Students (D-2 Visa):** Foreign students enrolled in Korean universities who held no substantial domestic taxable income qualify automatically under the indigent student standard.
   - **Surviving Dependent Heirs in Developing Nations:** Where the deceased foreign student's surviving parents reside in a developing country (Bangladesh) and earn agricultural/rural subsistence income well below the 125% median threshold, they qualify unconditionally for 100% free legal aid.
   - **Traffic Accident Fatalities:** Victims of motor vehicle collisions and their statutory successors claiming first-party insurance benefits under the Commercial Code are granted priority legal aid intake.
3. **Legal Standing of Overseas Heirs via Attorney-in-Fact:**
   - Under Korean Civil Procedure Act Arts. 87 and 89 and Private International Law Act Art. 49, overseas heirs (Belal Hossain and Monoara Begum) who cannot travel to South Korea are lawfully represented by their appointed Attorney-in-Fact (HASIBUL MUNIM (NID: 6003330534), Authorized Attorney-in-Fact for Legal Heirs) through a consular-legalized Special Power of Attorney (소송대리 특별위임장).
