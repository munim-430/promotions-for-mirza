### 2.2 Verbatim Match & Inter-Page Perforation Stamp (간인/契印) Rules

1. **Verbatim Parity Rule (우편법 시행규칙 제46조 내지 제59조):**
   - The Sender Name (*발신인*), Recipient Name (*수신인*), Corporate Registration (*법인명 및 대표자*), and Postal Addresses on the document's page 1 header must match the mailing envelope character-for-character.
   - For DB Insurance HQ:
     - Header: `수신인: DB손해보험주식회사 대표이사 정종표`
     - Address: `서울특별시 강남구 테헤란로 432 (대치동, DB금융센터) [우편번호: 06194]`
     - Envelope: Verbatim matching `(우편번호 06194) 서울특별시 강남구 테헤란로 432, DB금융센터 DB손해보험주식회사 대표이사 정종표 귀하`.
2. **Inter-Page Seal (간인 / 契印) Execution:**
   - Where a notice spans 2 or more pages, fold the right margin over the subsequent page and affix the legal representative's personal seal (*인감도장*) or authorized signet across the intersection.
   - Alternatively, use fan-fold stamping across all page edges.

1. **Delivery Confirmation Service (*배달증명 - Postal Service Rules Art. 59*):**
   - Korea Post formally records the exact delivery date, hour, and the recipient's signature/stamp upon delivery, mailing an official Delivery Certificate Postcard (*배달증명통지서*) back to the sender.
   - **Critical Legal Function:** Provides indisputable, court-certified proof of the exact service arrival date (*도달일자*), triggering statutory default interest and the 10-day provisional payment deadline under Civil Act Article 111 (도달주의).
2. **Next-Day Express (*익일특급*):**
   - Guarantees delivery to the recipient within 24 hours of dispatch, accelerating the start of the 10-day countdown.

### 3.3 Dual-Stage Default Interest Engine (Commercial 6.0% -> Litigation 12.0% p.a.)

1. **Stage 1: Commercial Default Interest (상사법정이율 연 6.0%)**
   - **Legal Anchor:** Korean Commercial Code Article 54 (*상법 제54조 상사법정이율*).
   - **Accrual Window:** From Day 11 following delivery of the Content-Certified provisional demand until the date of service of the formal civil complaint.
   - **Daily Calculation:** $\text{Daily Interest} = \text{Principal Amount} \times \frac{0.06}{365}$.

2. **Stage 2: Special Act Litigation Interest (소송촉진특례법상 연 12.0%)**
   - **Legal Anchor:** Special Act on Promotion of Litigation, etc. Article 3(1) (*소송촉진 등에 관한 특례법 제3조 제1항*).
   - **Accrual Window:** From the day following service of the formal civil complaint upon DB Insurance until full satisfaction and payment.
   - **Daily Calculation:** $\text{Daily Interest} = \text{Principal Amount} \times \frac{0.12}{365}$.

### 5.2 Foreign Exchange Bank Designation & Non-Taxation Clearance

   - Under Korean Foreign Exchange Transactions Regulations Chapter 4, the Attorney-in-Fact or Insurer registers the designated transaction at a major foreign exchange bank (e.g., Hana Bank World Center Branch or Shinhan Bank Global Desk).

2. **Inheritance & Gift Tax Exemption Confirmation (*상속세 및 증여세법 검토*):**
   - Under Korean Supreme Court precedent (2003Da29463), first-party accidental death benefits are the **inherent property of the heirs (상속인의 고유재산)**.
   - Statutory Basic Exemption: Under the Inheritance and Gift Tax Act, basic deductions of KRW 200,000,000 apply. Because total recovery proceeds (KRW 110,000,000 ~ 150,000,000) are below the KRW 200M deduction, zero Korean inheritance tax liability arises.
   - The Foreign Exchange Bank issues the **Non-Taxable Outward Remittance Verification Certificate (비과세 대외송금 확인서)**.
