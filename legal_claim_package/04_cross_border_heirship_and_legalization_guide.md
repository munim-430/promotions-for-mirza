## 2. PRIVATE INTERNATIONAL LAW ART. 49 & BANGLADESHI SUNNI HANAFI SUCCESSION ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 Choice of Law under Korean Private International Law Act Article 49
Under the conflict of laws rules of the Republic of Korea, the devolution of estate assets and the legal status of statutory heirs are determined exclusively by **Article 49 of the Private International Law Act (국제사법 제49조)**:

```
[국제사법 제49조 (상속)]
① 상속은 사망 당시 피상속인의 본국법에 의한다.
② 피상속인은 유언에 정하는 방식에 따라 다음 각 호의 어느 하나에 해당하는 법을 지정하여 상속을 준거법으로 할 수 있다. 다만, 유류분에 관한 권리는 피상속인의 사망 당시 본국법에 의한다.
   1. 지정 당시 피상속인의 상거소가 있는 국가의 법
   2. 부동산에 관한 상속의 경우 그 부동산이 소재하는 국가의 법
```

#### Legal Mechanics:
1. **Intestate Lex Patriae (본국법주의)**: The deceased Mahadi Monoar Hossain executed no testamentary document designating Korean law or habitual residence law under Article 49(2). Therefore, pursuant to Article 49(1), **the national law of Bangladesh (피상속인의 본국법인 방글라데시 법률)** exclusively governs all questions of succession.
2. **Universal Scope of the Governing Law**: The lex successionis determines:
   - The capacity to inherit and priority order of legal heirs (상속인의 범위 및 상속순위);
   - The fractional mathematical apportionment of inheritance shares (상속지분율);
   - The devolution of claims, causes of action, and accidental death benefit rights.

### 2.2 Substantive Bangladeshi Muslim Succession Law (Sunni Hanafi School)
In Bangladesh, succession for Muslim citizens is governed by personal religious law pursuant to **The Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937** and **The Succession Act, 1925**. The majority of Bangladeshi Muslims belong to the **Sunni Hanafi School of Jurisprudence** (*Sirajiyyah* principles of inheritance).

#### Hanafi Classification of Heirs:
1. **Sharers (*Zawil Furud* / 12 Quranic Heirs)**: Entitled to fixed statutory fractions designated in the Holy Quran.
2. **Residuaries (*Asaba* / Agnatic Heirs)**: Entitled to take the entire residue of the estate after Sharers receive their prescribed shares.
3. **Distant Kindred (*Zawil Arham*)**: Blood relations who inherit only if there are no living Sharers or Residuaries.

- **Deceased**: MAHADI MONOAR HOSSAIN (Male, Unmarried, Intestate).
- **Spouse**: None (Never married).
- **Descendants (*Awlad*)**: None (No children or grandchildren).
- **Ascendants**:
  - Father: **BELAL HOSSAIN** (Alive).
  - Mother: **MONOARA BEGUM** (Alive).
  - Grandparents: Excluded by living parents.
- **Collaterals**: Siblings (if any) are completely excluded (*Mahjub*) by the presence of the living Father.

### 3.1 International Legal Context & Institutional Realities in South Korea
1. **International Treaty Status**:
   - The Republic of Korea acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention on October 25, 2006 (effective July 14, 2007).
   - The People Republic of Bangladesh acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention in July 2023.
2. **Institutional Practicalities in South Korea**:
   - Although a Hague Apostille issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), Dhaka, has full formal validity under international law, Korean commercial claims adjusters at DB Insurance, compliance officers at foreign exchange banks, and the Financial Supervisory Service (FSS) often apply conservative internal scrutiny to documents from newly acceding jurisdictions.
3. **The Dual-Track Solution**:

   - **Track B (Consular Legalization Route)**: The gold standard for financial payouts and litigation. Certified by MOFA Dhaka and legalized by the **Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Dhaka, Bangladesh (주방글라데시 대한민국 대사관)** pursuant to Article 30 of the Overseas Missions Notarial Act. ### 3.2 Detailed Step-by-Step Execution Protocols in Dhaka

#### Track A: Hague Apostille Step-by-Step Protocol
1. **Step 1 - Drafting & Verification**: Draft the instrument (e.g., Bilingual Special POA) and assemble government certificates (Legal Heir Certificate, Family Relationship Certificate).
2. **Step 2 - Notarization**: Execute the documents before a licensed Notary Public in Dhaka/Cumilla. The Notary verifies identity via National ID Cards (NID) and affixes the official notarial seal and certificate.
3. **Step 3 - MOFA Apostille Issuance**: Submit the notarized dossier to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), Consular & Official Documents Section, Segunbagicha, Dhaka. MOFA affixes the standardized Hague Apostille Certificate with a unique verification QR code / serial number.
4. **Step 4 - Dispatch to Korea**: Courier original Apostilled instruments to the appointed representative in Korea via DHL/FedEx.

#### Track B: Consular Legalization (전통적 영사확인) Step-by-Step Protocol
1. **Step 1 - Notarization**: Execution and verification before a licensed Notary Public in Bangladesh.
2. **Step 2 - MOFA Bangladesh Consular Attestation**: Submission to MOFA Dhaka for official departmental authentication and signature verification of the Notary Public.
3. **Step 3 - Korean Embassy Consular Legalization**:
   - Submit the MOFA-attested document to the Consular Section of the **Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh** (Address: Madani Avenue, Baridhara, Dhaka-1212; Tel: +880-2-5881-2088).
   - The Korean Consul examines the document and affixes the official Consular Legalization Stamp (영사확인 직인) pursuant to Article 30 of the Republic of Korea Notarial Act for Overseas Missions.
4. **Step 4 - Dispatch to Korea**: Courier the consularly legalized originals to Korea.
