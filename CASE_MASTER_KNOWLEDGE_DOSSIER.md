### 3.1 Korean Commercial Code (상법 / KCC)
1. **Article 732-2 (Accidental Death / Injury Caused by Gross Negligence):**
   > *"In cases of insurance contracts where death or injury is an insured event, even if the insured event occurred due to the gross negligence of the policyholder, insured, or beneficiary, the insurer shall NOT be exempt from paying the insurance proceeds."*
   - **Legal Effect:** DB Insurance Co., Ltd. is strictly prohibited by statute from denying death benefits on grounds of unlicensed driving (무면허 운전) or gross negligence by the deceased.
2. **Article 663 (Prohibition of Disadvantageous Alteration / 불이익변경금지원칙):**
   > *"Special terms and conditions disadvantageous to policyholders, insured persons, or beneficiaries in violation of this Part shall be invalid."*
   - **Legal Effect:** Any exclusion clause in DB Insurance's standard terms that attempts to exclude unlicensed driving from accidental death benefits is legally null and void *ab initio*.
3. **Article 739 (Application to Accident Insurance):**
   - Applies the mandatory statutory protections of life insurance directly to accident insurance contracts.
4. **Article 54 (Commercial Legal Interest Rate):**
   - Imposes a mandatory statutory interest rate of **6.0% per annum** on delayed insurance proceeds.

### 3.2 Automobile Insurance Standard Policy (자동차보험 표준약관)
- **Article 34 (Payment of Insurance Proceeds & Provisional Advance):**
  - Insurers must pay confirmed claims within **10 business days** of receiving required documentation.
  - If claim investigation is prolonged, the insurer is statutorily mandated to pay a **Provisional Advance (가지급금) of at least 50%** of the estimated insurance benefit (KRW 50,000,000) within 10 business days of application.

### 3.3 Landmark Supreme Court Precedents
1. **Supreme Court En Banc Decision 98Da48963 (Decided Dec 28, 1998 - Landmark Ruling):**
   - *Holding:* Self-Physical Accident coverage is first-party insurance designed to provide absolute financial security to passengers and drivers. The insurer cannot deny payout for accidental death even if the driver operated the vehicle unlicensed (무면허운전) or exhibited severe negligence. Exclusion clauses attempting to defeat Article 732-2 are void under Article 663.
2. **Supreme Court Decision 97Da27039 (Decided Sept 22, 1997):**
   - *Holding:* "Intentional act" (고의) requires subjective desire and purpose to cause one's own death (suicide). It cannot be inferred from risky or unlicensed driving conduct.
3. **Supreme Court Decision 98Da26910 (Decided Jan 15, 1999):**
   - *Holding:* First-party accident insurance benefits belong directly to the statutory heirs of the deceased as their inherent property (고유재산) and are independent of tortfeasor liabilities.

### 3.4 Procedural & Special Statutes
- **Financial Consumer Protection Act (금융소비자보호법) Arts. 28, 36~43:** Governs mandatory document disclosure requests (Art. 28) and FSS Dispute Mediation Committee complaints (Arts. 36~43), forcing regulatory oversight and suspending statutory extinctive prescription.

- **Legal Aid Act (법률구조법) Art. 22:** Grants free legal aid, court cost exemption, and assigned trial counsel to indigent foreign nationals.
